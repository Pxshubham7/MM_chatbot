# Grounding Techniques for Emotional Distress

Grounding exercises are non-clinical, practical techniques that help reconnect you with the present moment when experiencing overwhelming emotions, anxiety, or intrusive thoughts.

## 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Technique
When experiencing acute emotional overwhelm, look around your environment and quietly acknowledge:
1. **5 things you can see**: Notice small details like a shadow, a pattern on the wall, or light reflecting on a surface.
2. **4 things you can physically feel**: Notice the texture of your clothing, the solid surface of the floor beneath your feet, or the temperature of the air on your hands.
3. **3 things you can hear**: Listen for ambient sounds—birds chirping outside, a distant hum of traffic, or the sound of your own quiet breathing.
4. **2 things you can smell**: Notice any nearby aromas, such as coffee, fresh air, or clean laundry.
5. **1 thing you can taste**: Focus on a lingering taste in your mouth, or take a sip of cool water and focus on the sensation.

## The Physical Touch Reset
Hold an object with a distinct tactile sensation, such as a smooth stone, a piece of ice, or a textured fabric. Focus entirely on its temperature, weight, and texture. This helps anchor attention away from cognitive spiral and back into physical reality.
