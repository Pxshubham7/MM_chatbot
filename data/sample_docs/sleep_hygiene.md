# Non-Clinical Rest & Sleep Hygiene Guidelines

Adequate sleep and restorative rest provide essential foundations for emotional resilience. When feeling stressed or mentally exhausted, small environmental adjustments can support restorative rest.

## Evening Wind-Down Routine
1. **Reduce Bright and Blue Light**: Lower indoor lighting 60 minutes before sleep and disconnect from interactive screens (phones, computers, television).
2. **Brain Dump Journaling**: If anxious thoughts or tomorrow's to-do items keep your mind active, spend 5 minutes writing them down on a physical notepad to release working memory tension.
3. **Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)**: Starting from your toes up to your forehead, gently tense each muscle group for 5 seconds, then deliberately release the tension for 15 seconds. Notice the sensation of warmth and relaxation spreading through each area.

## Managing Nighttime Restlessness
If you are unable to fall asleep after approximately 20 to 30 minutes in bed:
- Avoid tossing and turning or checking the clock repeatedly.
- Move to a dimly lit, comfortable chair and engage in a calming, low-stimulation activity (such as reading a calm book or listening to ambient sounds).
- Return to bed only when physical drowsiness naturally returns.
