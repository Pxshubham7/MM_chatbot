# Structured Breathing Techniques for Nervous System Regulation

Controlled breathing exercises help stimulate the parasympathetic nervous system, gently reducing heart rate and physical tension during moments of stress.

## Box Breathing (Square Breathing)
Box breathing is a structured four-part breathing cycle:
1. **Inhale slowly** through your nose for a count of 4 seconds.
2. **Hold your breath** gently at the top for 4 seconds without straining.
3. **Exhale slowly** through your mouth for 4 seconds, releasing tension in your shoulders.
4. **Hold your breath** at the bottom for 4 seconds before the next breath.
Repeat this cycle 4 to 6 times to help restore calm and steady concentration.

## 4-7-8 Relaxing Breath
1. Place the tip of your tongue against the ridge behind your upper front teeth.
2. Inhale quietly through your nose to a mental count of 4.
3. Hold your breath gently for a count of 7.
4. Exhale completely through your mouth with a soft whooshing sound for a count of 8.
5. Repeat for up to 4 complete breath cycles.
This pattern encourages a longer exhalation, which naturally downregulates sympathetic physiological arousal.
